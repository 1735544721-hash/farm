Abstract

In the agricultural product sales chain, fragmented information, volatile inventory that is hard to trace, and opaque order fulfillment often reduce operational efficiency, while manual records cannot support scaled and refined management. To address these issues, this project designs and implements an agricultural product sales system. The backend is built with Spring Boot to provide REST APIs, MyBatis-Plus is used for data access and pagination, and MySQL serves as the core data store, with Spring Security and JWT ensuring authentication and role-based authorization. The system targets consumers, merchants, and administrators, and provides product and category management, product listing and inventory maintenance, shopping cart and order processing, Alipay payment, logistics tracking, address management, as well as information notices, carousel display, reviews, and favorites. User behavior and recommendation interfaces are also included to support operational optimization. The design links inbound and outbound stock with order processing to keep inventory quantities consistent with order states. Functional tests cover registration and login, product management, order payment, stock in and out, and logistics sign-off. Results show stable operation and good data consistency, meeting the business requirements of online agricultural sales and inventory control, while laying a foundation for future expansion such as multi-warehouse coordination and data analysis. Keywords: agricultural product sales; inventory management; Spring Boot; MySQL; order logistics
